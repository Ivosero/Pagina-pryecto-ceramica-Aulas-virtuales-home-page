About This Course

This tactile, hands-on masterclass is crafted to transform your relationship with clay. Over six weeks, you will learn the essential choreography of wheel throwing. We move beyond simple instruction into the sensory experience of shaping earth, focusing on intention, posture, and the subtle dialogues between hand and material.

Whether you are looking for a meditative creative outlet or aspiring to build functional ceramics for daily use, this foundation will give you the confidence and technique to realize your vision.

What You'll Master

check_circle Consistent centering of up to 3lbs of clay
check_circle Pulling even, thin walls for cylinders and bowls
check_circle Trimming perfect foot rings with precision tools
check_circle Basic dipping and pouring glazing techniques

Curriculum Overview

Module 1 The Studio & The Wheel
expand_more
Introduction to studio safety, wheel anatomy, clay preparation, and proper wedging techniques to remove air bubbles before throwing.
Module 2 The Art of Centering
expand_more
Mastering body mechanics to center clay effortlessly. We will focus on coning up and down, and establishing a solid, stable base.
Module 3 Opening & Pulling Cylinders
expand_more
The foundational shape of pottery. Learn to open the center, compress the bottom, and execute even pulls to create tall, uniform walls.
