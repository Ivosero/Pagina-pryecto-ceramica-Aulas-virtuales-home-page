Meet the Maker

Founded by master ceramicist Elena Rostova, our studio was born from a desire to share the profound, grounding experience of working with earth.

With over two decades of experience spanning traditional Japanese wood-firing techniques to contemporary minimalist design, Elena brings a wealth of knowledge and a gentle, encouraging teaching philosophy to every session.
